Our Story

Jagat means “the world”.

We believe the small things we carry with us shape how we move through it. Each Jagat bracelet brings together stones chosen for one intention, strung by hand and finished with the lotus, a symbol of growth that rises clear from muddy water.

Caring for your bracelet

Keep it dry

Remove before swimming, bathing or exercise to protect the stones and the stretch cord.

Roll, don't pull

Roll the bracelet over your hand instead of stretching it on and off.

Cleanse often

Rest it on selenite or in moonlight overnight to reset its energy.
